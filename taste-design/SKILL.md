# Expressive design preset

Treat this as an optional direction: generous contrast, deliberate asymmetry,
characterful typography, and purposeful motion. The brief and incumbent product
identity override every suggestion. Use `impeccable` for general design decisions
and `design-md` to document an existing Stitch design.

Choose palette, density, type, and composition for the content. No typeface,
serif family, black color, centered hero, or number of accents is universally
banned. Do not insert decorative imagery or invented claims merely to fill a
preset. A useful dashboard can be quiet and dense.

Motion should communicate state or guide attention. Avoid endless motion by
default, support reduced motion, and keep controls usable without animation.
Adapt responsive layouts to the actual content rather than mandating a single
mobile layout. Validate interaction and accessibility in the implemented surface.
